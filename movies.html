Movie Recommendations

Surprise! A Creative IT student obsessed with movies. It is a family tradition to watch films and deeply discuss them after. This taste grew on me and I hope you enjoy my principal recommendations, purely based on my biased and non-objective opinions.

The Matrix Saga

The Matrix saga follows Neo, a computer hacker who discovers that reality is a simulated world created by intelligent machines to control humanity while using their bodies as an energy source.

Chosen as "The One," Neo joins a rebellion to free minds from the Matrix, confronting powerful programs like Agent Smith and uncovering deeper layers of control. As the war between humans and machines escalates, Neo sacrifices himself to bring peace.

Years later, both he and Trinity are resurrected in a new version of the Matrix, where they reclaim their freedom and power to reshape reality on their terms.

 
Director: Lana Wachowski, Lilly Wachowski.

Dark Shadows

In the 18th century, wealthy playboy Barnabas Collins breaks the heart of the witch Angelique Bouchard, who curses him into becoming a vampire and buries him alive.

Nearly 200 years later, Barnabas is accidentally freed in 1972 and returns to his ancestral home to find his once-powerful family in disarray.

As he tries to restore the Collins family fortune and adapt to the strange new world of the 1970s, Barnabas must face Angelique again—still alive, powerful, and hellbent on revenge.

 
Director: Tim Burton.

ParaNorman

In the town of Blithe Hollow, misunderstood and outcasted young Norman Babcock has the unique ability to see and speak to ghosts.

When a centuries-old witch's curse threatens to raise the dead and wreak havoc on the town, Norman must use his powers to uncover the truth behind the curse and confront both supernatural forces and the fears of the living.

With heart and humour, ParaNorman blends spooky adventure with a powerful message about acceptance, courage, and standing up for what’s right.

 
Director: Chris Butler, Sam Fell.

Pan's Labyrinth

Set in post–Civil War Spain in 1944, Pan’s Labyrinth follows a young girl named Ofelia, who escapes the harsh realities of her new life under her cruel stepfather, a fascist military officer, by entering a mysterious and magical labyrinth.

There, a faun tells her she may be the reincarnation of a lost princess, if she can complete three dangerous tasks.

Blending fantasy and brutal historical drama, the film explores themes of innocence, rebellion, and the power of imagination in the face of tyranny.

 
Director: Guillermo del Toro.
